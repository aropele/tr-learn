# eiopa.europa.eu: publications to ingest

Backlog of European Insurance and Occupational Pensions Authority (EIOPA) publications on measured costs and past performance of insurance-based investment products (IBIPs, incl. unit-linked) and personal pension products, for the pension-fund-vs-ETF theme. Collected 2026-10-16 from the server-rendered document library
[reports and research](https://www.eiopa.europa.eu/document-library/reports-and-research_en) (pages 1-13 of 17, `?page=0..12`, back to 2016: 14 of 260 items kept),
plus publication pages reached from EIOPA news items and site search, because supervisory statements, opinions and PEPP material sit outside that listing: value for money (4), IORP costs and charges (2), PEPP technical standards and letter (2).
Left out: Solvency II prudential and reporting, stress tests, financial stability, climate/NatCat, DORA/ICT, IDD sanctions and application reports, IBIP mystery shopping (sales process, not costs), IORPs in Focus and cross-border IORP reports (market structure, no cost data in the description), Consumer Trends 2020-2022 (COVID and financial health, no cost focus in the description), PEPP impact assessment, consumer testing and stochastic model.

EIOPA is the EU supervisor for insurance and occupational pensions and sells nothing: ingest with `--publisher eiopa` (institutional, no `--popular`). Its mandate leans to consumer protection, and it designed and promotes the PEPP, so its PEPP material argues for the product and its cost reports stress poor value for money. URLs are direct PDFs (`/document/download/<uuid>_en?filename=...`); the publication page is named in the caveats when it matters. The site serves plain HTML with no consent wall seen, so `--file` should not be needed. When a row is archived, set Status to its id.

- Dates are publication-page dates. Files on most pages carry a `31 January 2023` stamp from the site migration: that is not the publication date. Only the 2026 report (`EIOPA-BoS-26/122`, 01 April 2026) and the 2015 IORP report (`EIOPA-BoS-14/266`, 7 January 2015) were checked against the PDF; take the others' date from the PDF before running `add.py`.
- Two editions carry 2023 in the title: "Costs and past performance report 2023" (page date 2023-01-17, costs for 2021) and "EIOPA's Costs and Past Performance Report December 2023" (2023-12-18, costs for 2022). There is no edition titled 2024; the 2025 and 2026 editions follow.
- The 2020 edition page (https://www.eiopa.europa.eu/publications/cost-and-past-performance-2020-report_en) lists only a "corrigendum" PDF (3.56 MB). Confirm it is the full corrected report and not an errata sheet; if not, search order: other official URL > third-party copy > Wayback.
- Dead URL: the 2020 news item links https://www.eiopa.europa.eu/content/cost-and-past-performance-2020-report_en, which returns 404 (old `/content/` paths). The generic slug https://www.eiopa.europa.eu/publications/costs-and-past-performance-report_en serves the 2025 edition (duplicate, not listed).
- The 2024 value-for-money benchmark methodology is marked "[OUTDATED]" and replaced by the 2026 revision; keep both (the 2024 one is cited by NCA work in between). Its page now also lives at `/publications/outdated-methodology-value-money-benchmarks_en`.
- The 2015 IORP report is on the old register host (`register.eiopa.europa.eu`), not the main site.
- Italy: the 2026 report mentions Italy once (p. 4, short average holding periods) and has a PPP section, but never names PIPs, fondi pensione or COVIP. Whether earlier editions give country-level rows for Italy is unverified; check each PDF while transcribing. Italian unit-linked PIPs fall under IBIPs/PPPs in EIOPA's scope; Italian fondi pensione negoziali are IORPs (only the two IORP rows cover them).
- The PEPP RTS PDF bundles several delegated acts; the cost-cap part (types of costs and fees under the 1% Basic PEPP cap) is one section of it.
