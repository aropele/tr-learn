# bancaditalia.it: publications to ingest

Backlog of Banca d'Italia publications for the pension-fund-vs-ETF theme (supplementary pensions, household portfolio choice and saving, fund costs, financial literacy), collected 2026-10-16.
The two paper series were crawled through the site's own search form (title-and-abstract keyword search, all years, every results page): [Questioni di Economia e Finanza (Occasional Papers)](https://www.bancaditalia.it/pubblicazioni/qef/index.html) (27 of 145 hits kept) and [Temi di discussione (Working Papers)](https://www.bancaditalia.it/pubblicazioni/temi-discussione/index.html) (19 of 280 hits). Survey reports came from the [Indagine sui bilanci delle famiglie](https://www.bancaditalia.it/pubblicazioni/indagine-famiglie/index.html) index (3 of 20 editions, the latest three) and the [indagini sull'alfabetizzazione finanziaria](https://www.bancaditalia.it/pubblicazioni/indagini-alfabetizzazione/index.html) index (2 of 3). Keywords used: pension/previdenza/TFR/annuity, portfolio/risparmio/funds/fondi/ETF/fees, literacy/alfabetizzazione/educazione/consulenza.
Left out: monetary policy, banking supervision and bank lending, macro and labour-market papers that matched only on "pension" or "portfolio", institutional-investor and fund-fragility papers, school and workplace financial-education evaluations, microenterprise literacy, the small-business literacy survey, and IBF editions before 2016.

Banca d'Italia is a central bank with nothing to sell: ingest with `--publisher banca-ditalia` (institutional, no `--popular`). Each URL is the PDF, which is the original. The publication pages are server-rendered, so `--file` should not be needed. Under the Italian page, an English paper shows "(solo in inglese)" and an `en_` filename. QEF papers are mostly in Italian with an English page title; the Italian title printed in the PDF is the one kept here. Each paper carries the series disclaimer: the views are the authors', not the Bank's. When a row is archived, set Status to its id.

- Dead URL: search order is another bancaditalia.it URL (the publication page `.../index.html` next to the PDF, or the `?language_id=1` English copy) > third-party copy (RePEc/IDEAS, SSRN) > Wayback (`/web/<ts>id_/<url>`).
- Dates are the month printed on each publication page (Temi pages also print it on the PDF cover). The search listing gives day-level dates, but many of them are placeholders (end of month, batch days). Confirm the month on the PDF before `add.py`. The survey reports carry no date on the page: their month comes from the search listing (IBF 2022: 16 Ottobre 2024; IBF 2016: 12 Marzo 2018, also on the PDF cover; adult literacy 2023: 20 Luglio 2023).
- Everything here predates the EUR 5,300 deduction ceiling (from tax year 2026). Papers on taxation (QEF 464, Temi 938, Temi 349) describe the regime of their year: 12.5% on financial income before 2012, 20% in 2012–14, 26% since July 2014.
- QEF 8 (2007) and QEF 77 (2010) describe the 2007 TFR reform (silenzio-assenso), not the 2026 auto-enrolment rules.
- Temi 503 and 349 are Italian-only PDFs, and 349's text layer is garbled (font encoding). Extract it with OCR, or check it against the page.
- IBF 2020 also links a methodological note (`Enhancing_the_SHIW_weighting_methodology_with_external_data_sources.pdf`), and IBF 2016 links `MOP_IBF.pdf`. These are not listed. Only the main fascicolo is.
