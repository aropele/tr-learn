# rpc.cfainstitute.org: publications to ingest

Backlog of CFA Institute Research Foundation publications relevant to portfolio construction, collected 2026-10-16. The [publications catalog](https://rpc.cfainstitute.org/research-foundation/publications) builds its list with JavaScript (Coveo), so items came from the server-rendered [sitemap](https://rpc.cfainstitute.org/sitemap.xml) (all pages): 91 of the 304 `/research/foundation/` pages kept (monographs, briefs, literature reviews, SBBI data books), each page and PDF opened. Titles, dates and authors come from each page's `citation_*` metadata; affiliations come from the PDF title page or author bio.
Left out: ESG and climate, ethics, governance and investment committees, pension-system policy, market microstructure, accounting and valuation, single-security analysis, AI/ML/fintech, crypto, behavioral-finance essays, regional capital-market surveys, annual reviews, errata, and the four section PDFs of *Exponential Wealth* (the full monograph is listed). Financial Analysts Journal articles and In Practice briefs are out of scope (they are not RF publications).

The Research Foundation is a not-for-profit with nothing to sell: ingest with `--publisher cfa-institute-research-foundation` (institutional, no `--popular`). The same folder covers its earlier names (Research Foundation of CFA Institute, of AIMR, of the Institute of Chartered Financial Analysts, Financial Analysts Research Foundation). Its authors are practitioners, and many work for firms that sell the strategy or product they write about. The `Author affiliation` column shows that per row; carry it into `--notes`. URLs are publication pages, and the PDF linked from each page is the original. When a row is archived, set Status to its id.

- **No paywall:** every row except 1996 has a free PDF on its page, and all 91 pages and 90 PDFs returned 200 on 2026-10-16 (scripted `curl` works, no `--file` needed). Older pages also link legacy `cfainstitute.org/-/media/...` PDF URLs; use the `rpc.cfainstitute.org/sites/default/files/...` link.
- **Pick the right PDF** where a page links several: 2015 ETF guide (English `rf-v2015-n3-1-pdf.pdf`, not the summary or Portuguese/Chinese translations), 2018 alternatives primer (`rf-v2018-n1-1.pdf`, not the Japanese translation), 2015 longevity review (`rflr-v10-n2-1-pdf.pdf` is the full 106 pp; `rflr-v10-n2-1.pdf` is an 8 pp summary), 2013 ALM review (full, not `-sum`), 2026 ETF Module 2 (`rf_etfs_module2_...`, the page also links Module 1), 2019 cash-flow brief (plus appendix A PDF).
- **Confirm dates from the PDF.** Exact days exist only from 2019 on. Before that, the CMS stamps day `01` (shown here as `YYYY-MM`) or `01-01` (shown as `YYYY`, e.g. 2006 is "corrected April 2006" in the PDF, 1987's foreword is dated December 1987).
- **Titles are as shown on the page,** with the site's "(Full Monograph PDF)"/"(Full PDF)" suffix and "| RPC" dropped. Where the page title is shorter than the PDF's, use the PDF's: TPA review ("...: A Critical Literature Review"), McCaffrey 2026 ("...: Dividends and 'The Return Nobody Got'"), Factor Investing and Asset Allocation ("...: A Business Cycle Perspective"), ETF Modules 1–2 ("A Comprehensive Guide to ETFs (2nd edition)"). The 2009 life-cycle title takes its subtitle from the PDF to tell it apart from 2007.
- **Two pages share a title:** "Revisiting the Equity Risk Premium" 2023 (the 126 pp forum book) and 2024 (Ibbotson's 5 pp presentation from the same forum). Ingest the 2024 one only if a separate id is wanted.
- **1996 Currency Management (Clarke, Kritzman):** the page links no PDF. Before ingesting, search per the CLAUDE.md order: another CFA URL, then a third-party copy, then Wayback.
- **Pre-2000 PDFs are scans** with a poor text layer (1998 Gorman, 1995 Reichenstein–Dorsett: affiliation unreadable). Read the page images when transcribing.
- **No dead URLs** at collection time. If one dies later, follow the "Finding a source" order and put the dead URL in `--notes`.
